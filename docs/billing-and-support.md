# Billing and support

The Team plan costs 12 dollars per user each month, billed monthly or yearly. Yearly billing gives a 20 percent discount.

You can cancel at any time from the Billing page. After cancellation your data remains available for 30 days before it is deleted.

Support is available by email at all times. Team plan customers also get live chat from Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 UTC.

If a device has not connected for 60 days, Acme sends a warning email. After 90 days without a connection the backups for that device are paused.
