# Acme Cloud Backup: Getting started

Acme Cloud Backup keeps encrypted copies of your files in the cloud. Install the desktop agent, sign in, and choose the folders to protect.

The free plan includes 10 GB of storage for one device. The Team plan includes 1 TB shared between up to 25 devices.

By default the agent runs a backup every hour. You can change the schedule to every 15 minutes, daily or weekly in Settings, then Schedule.

Files are encrypted on your device with AES-256 before they are uploaded. Only you hold the encryption key, so Acme staff cannot read your files.
