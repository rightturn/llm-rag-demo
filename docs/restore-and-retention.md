# Restoring files and retention

To restore a file, open the web console, select the device, and choose Restore. You can restore a single file, a folder, or an entire device.

Deleted files stay recoverable for 30 days on the free plan and for 180 days on the Team plan. After the retention period, deleted files are permanently removed.

Previous versions of a changed file are kept for 14 days on the free plan. The Team plan keeps versions for 90 days.

Large restores over 50 GB can be shipped on an encrypted drive. Contact support to request a restore drive; delivery takes 5 to 7 business days.
